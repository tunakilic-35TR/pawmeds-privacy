Privacy Policy — Last Updated: April 3, 2026

About

PawMeds is a mobile application that helps you track your pets' medications. Your privacy is important to us.

Data We Collect

  • Pet Information: Name, species, age, weight, veterinarian name
  • Medication Information: Medicine name, dosage, schedule, notes
  • User Preferences: Language selection, notification settings
  • Ad Data: Anonymous advertising ID collected by Google AdMob

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device only. No data is sent to any server or cloud system.

Data Sharing

Your personal data is never shared with any third party.

Google AdMob

Our app uses Google AdMob to serve ads so we can offer the app for free. AdMob may use anonymous device identifiers for ad delivery.

For more information: Google Privacy Policy

Your Rights

  • View and edit your data
  • Delete your data (by uninstalling the app)
  • Disable notifications at any time
